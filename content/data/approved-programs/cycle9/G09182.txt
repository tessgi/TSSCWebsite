# TESS Program G09182

Title: A Five-Fold Increase In ``Holy Grail'' Calibrators: Addressing Uncertainties In Stellar Models With TESS

PI: Joyce, Meridith - University Of Wyoming

Type: SMALL

Summary: We propose to obtain 200-s cadence TESS FFIs for 156 wide binary systems likely to host detectable oscillations in both components. The targets are drawn from a Gaia wide binary catalog and have XGBoost temperatures, gravities, and metallicities. From TESS power spectra we will measure the asteroseismic quantities $\nu_{\max}$ and $\Delta\nu$, which combined with these physical parameters yield stellar masses via standard scaling relations. These masses, together with Gaia-based metallicities, provide the constraints needed to calibrate stellar models known as isochrones.
