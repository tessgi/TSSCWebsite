# TESS Program G09126

Title: TESS Cycle 9 Monitoring Of Young And Resonant Planetary Systems

PI: Dai, Fei - University Of Hawaii (Manoa)

Type: SMALL

Summary: We propose a Cycle 9 program to monitor ~85 planetary systems that can directly test the Break-the-Chains model of close-in planet formation. Our sample includes ~8 confirmed systems younger than 100 Myr, ~17 systems younger than 1 Gyr, and ~60 additional TTV systems that are likely young. Recent work suggests that young planetary systems are preferentially found near mean-motion resonances, but this picture is still based on only a small number of confirmed young systems. Cycle 9's longer coverage improves sensitivity to missed outer companions and extends the timing baseline for Kepler/K2 systems by up to a decade. We will search for additional transiting planets, identify resonant chains, constrain masses, orbital architectures, and ephemerides especially for the JWST targets
