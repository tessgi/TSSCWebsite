# TESS Program G09157

Title: Rotation Periods And X-Ray Fluxes Of F-Stars With TESS And Swift

PI: Wilson, David - University Of Colorado At Boulder

Type: SMALL

Summary: F-type stars are increasingly important as planet host targets for atmosphere escape studies today and as HWO targets in the future. However there are key gaps in our understanding of these stars due to the traditional focus on smaller stars. We propose to observe a sample of F stars with TESS and Swift, many of which are likely HWO targets, to provide accurate rotation periods and fill out the X-ray-Rossby number activity diagram.
