Title: Notice of call for community-requested targets: expanded TESS-SPOC FFI light curve processing
Date: 2026-10-19 00:00
Author: Amy Tuson

The <a href="https://archive.stsci.edu/hlsp/tess-spoc" target="_blank" rel="noopener">TESS-SPOC</a> pipeline is expanding its Full Frame Image (FFI) light curve target list by approximately 4x beginning with Sector 97.  

We invite the TESS community to submit lists of targets that they would like included in upcoming TESS-SPOC FFI processing, beginning with Sector 97 and continuing on an approximately annual cadence going forward. Submitted target lists will remain valid for three years, after which a renewed or updated submission may be requested. 

**Read the call <a href="docs/call-for-community-targets-2026.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a> and submit your priority ranked target list and brief justification <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc_4r1Nae67DSHCPbNEVCv9i9Xp0HoroJhMc39dMXOsYH7FZA/viewform" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a> by December 15, 2026.**

For questions about this call, please contact [tesshelp@bigbang.gsfc.nasa.gov](mailto:tesshelp@bigbang.gsfc.nasa.gov).
