Title: TESS Cycle 10 pointings are available now
Date: 2026-08-26 01:00
Author: Amy Tuson

The TESS Cycle 10 pointings are available now!

Cycle 10 spans 19 September 2027 to 13 September 2028. It is comprised of 13 sectors, each of which is approximately 27 days long. TESS will observe the Northern ecliptic hemisphere (sectors 122 to 132) and the ecliptic plane (sectors 133 and 134). The Cycle 10 sky coverage map is shown below. Full Frame Images (FFIs) will be collected at 200-second cadence and observations of pre-selected stars will be made at 2-minute or 20-second cadence.

The Cycle 10 pointings are available [here](https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/tess/sector.html) and on the [MIT TESS website](https://tess.mit.edu/observations/). They have also been added to the [`tesswcs`](https://github.com/tessgi/tesswcs) and [`tess-point`](https://github.com/tessgi/tess-point) Python packages.
