Title: Cycle 10 Call for Proposals: Deadline January 28 2027
Date: 2026-11-17 00:00
Author: Amy Tuson

The TESS Cycle 10 Call for Proposals has been released. **Proposals are due on January&nbsp;28,&nbsp;2027 at 4:30&nbsp;PM&nbsp;EST.** The Cycle 10 solicitation can be found as part of the <a href="https://nspires.nasaprs.com/external/solicitations/summary.do?solId=%7bA952E51B-7AD4-B180-2F8C-4E8A79AFFAED%7d&path=&method=init" target="_blank">D.3 Combined General Investigator General Observer Program</a>, with supplementary information available <a href="docs/TESS_Cycle10_V1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a> and on our [how to propose page](proposing-investigations.html).

Cycle 10 observations begin in September 2027 and end in September 2028, spanning observing sectors 122-134. TESS will observe the Northern ecliptic hemisphere (sectors 122 to 132) and the ecliptic plane (sectors 133 and 134). You can read more about the pointings [here](tess-cycle-10-pointings-are-available-now.html), on our [sectors page](sector.html) and the [MIT observations page](https://tess.mit.edu/observations/).
