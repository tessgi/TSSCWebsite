Cycle 10 Call for Proposals: Deadline January 28 2027

Date Tue 17 November 2026 By Amy Tuson

The TESS Cycle 10 Call for Proposals has been released. Proposals are due on January 28, 2027 at 4:30 PM EST. The Cycle 10 solicitation can be found as part of the D.3 Combined General Investigator General Observer Program, with supplementary information available here and on our how to propose page.

Cycle 10 observations begin in September 2027 and end in September 2028, spanning observing sectors 122-134. TESS will observe the Northern ecliptic hemisphere (sectors 122 to 132) and the ecliptic plane (sectors 133 and 134). You can read more about the pointings here, on our sectors page and the MIT observations page.
