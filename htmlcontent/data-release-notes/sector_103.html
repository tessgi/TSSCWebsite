    Sector Notes

    Noted Issue Description
    Rolled Sector: Sector 103 is the fifth of 9 overlapping “rolled” sectors that provide extended temporal coverage of the southern ecliptic’s mid-latitudes. This observing plan is designed to detect longer period planets than typical TESS pointings at latitudes that are more amenable to follow up observations.
    Spacecraft pointing: The pointing in Sector 103 was set at−46.0 degrees in ecliptic latitude. The instrument field-of-view was rotated by approximately 40 degrees covering a region of southern ecliptic mid-latitudes. Camera 1 and Camera 4 were used for guiding during half-orbit segment 217a. Camera 1 alone was used during 218a, while Camera 4 alone was used during half-orbit segments 217b and 218b.
    Scattered light: In Sector 103, the Earth introduces scattered light signals in all cameras near the beginning of orbits 217a and 218a and end of orbit 217b and 218b. The moon also affects Camera 1 at the end of orbit 217b and beginning of orbit 218b
